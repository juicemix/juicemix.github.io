# Privacy Policy for Rolandia

**Effective date: 26 October 2026**

This policy explains what information Rolandia (the "game"), made by
**Juicemix Labs** ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you
have. It applies to the Android version of the game distributed through
Google Play.

**In short:** we do not run servers, we do not ask for your name or email,
and we do not sell your data. Your progress is saved on your device.
Google provides the game's ads, payments and (if you use it) cloud save, and
Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can continue where you
left off. This includes your crowns, meat, unlocked gear and skills, pets,
settings, game statistics (such as runs played), and a record of purchases
you have made in the game. This data stays on your device. We do not
receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows **optional rewarded video ads**: you choose to watch one to
revive once per run or to claim a bonus of crowns. There are no forced ads.

Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, AdMob may
collect and process information such as your device's **advertising ID**,
device and app information, your IP address and approximate location, and
how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and your choices, ads
may be personalised.

- **Consent:** where the law requires it (for example in the European
  Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the game asks for
  your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are
  shown.
- **Your choices:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out
  of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or
  Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
- **Remove Ads:** the "No Ads, Same Rewards" purchase stops all ads in the
  game.

Learn more: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed entirely by **Google Play**. We never see or store
your payment details. To deliver what you bought, the game receives from
Google Play the product you bought, an order number and a purchase token,
and keeps a record of the purchase token on your device so that the same
purchase is never delivered twice and can be restored if you reinstall.

See the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Cloud save (Google Play Games Services)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game can store a copy of your
saved progress (the same information as in section 1) in **Saved Games** in
your Google account, so it can be restored on a new or reset device. Google
Play Games may also share your Play Games player ID and profile with the
game. You can use the game without signing in. You can delete saved game
data from your Google account in the Google Play Games settings.

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise
  location.
- We do not run our own servers or analytics, and we do not receive your
  saved progress.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Children

Rolandia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove
it.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its
data. Cloud saves are kept in your Google account until you delete them.
Data collected by Google (AdMob, Google Play, Play Games) is retained under
[Google's policies](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Because we do not
hold your data ourselves, deleting the game and its cloud save removes the
game's data; for Google-held data, use your Google account settings.

## 8. Security

Purchases and ads are handled over encrypted connections by Google's
services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we
do not transfer your game data to any server of our own.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds a feature. We
will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
